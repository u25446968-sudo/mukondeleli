WHO WE ARE

Professional protection. Practical security.

Established in 2017, Mukondeleli Protection Services (Pty) Ltd is a South African security and protection company committed to providing professional, reliable and effective security solutions to individuals, businesses and public-sector clients.

Our services cover armed and unarmed guarding, access control, patrols, executive and personal protection, private investigations, technical security solutions, CCTV and IT security services. We take a practical approach to security, developing solutions around the specific risks, environment and requirements of every client.

At Mukondeleli, we believe effective security is built on discipline, vigilance, professionalism and trust. We also place strong emphasis on developing our personnel by empowering junior staff with valuable skills and knowledge, while maintaining a high standard of service and value for our clients.

Our goal is simple: to protect people, property and businesses through dependable security solutions that give our clients confidence.
