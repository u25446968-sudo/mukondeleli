WHO WE ARE

Innovating future of security.

Mukondeleli Protection Services (Pty) Ltd (est. 2017) is a proudly South African, 100% Black-owned security solutions company built to provide high-quality, professional and cost-effective security solutions worldwide.

OUR STORY

Built on trust. Driven by duty.

Mukondeleli Protection Services (Pty) Ltd is a Proprietary Limited Company registered under the Companies' Law of the Republic of South Africa, providing armed and unarmed security services to both private and public sector clients. We are registered with the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSIRA), mandated under the PSIRA Act 58 of 2001.

We deliver prime services as a professional and cost-effective security solution. As a 100% Black-owned company, we take pride in creating customised solutions — from Guarding and CCTV Monitoring to K9 Patrolling, Bodyguards/VIP/CPO, Riot Officers and Advanced Special Operations.
