GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

What we look for.

  • Suitable relevant educational standard and experience
  • Appropriate age and physical fitness
  • Registration with PSIRA and possession of security training certificates
  • Security clearance through SAPS
  • Numerate, computer literate, and able to read, write and communicate in English and other commonly used official languages
  • Willingness to undergo induction and refresher training programs
  • Willingness to work shifts, including nights and weekends

All staff are uniformed in line with the image of our clients, and are provided with transport and accommodation next to the sites, where required.
